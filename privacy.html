Fold Merge · for iPhone Duo

Privacy policy

Last updated November 1, 2026.

Fold Merge collects no personal data. It has no account, advertising, analytics or tracking, and it works offline.

What stays on your device

The app stores your current game, best score, preferences, lesson progress and tutorial completion on your device. It does not send this information to the developer or anyone else.

Hinge and timing

The app reads the device's hinge state to respond to folding, and measures elapsed time locally to avoid duplicate turns. It does not store or transmit hinge readings or timing information.

Permissions

Fold Merge does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.

Backups and deletion

Your operating system may include local app data in device backups according to your settings. Uninstalling the app removes its local data.
